Plan the light and the work that makes it possible

A lighting project may involve fixture replacement, new wiring points or a different control layout. The contractor needs to assess existing wiring, mounting support, equipment suitability and access. Fixture appearance alone does not establish compatibility with a dimmer or the suitability of a location. Share product information and the intended room use before installation is priced.

Prepare the scope for your Courtice property

Collect the instruction sheets for each chosen fixture and list where it will sit, such as above a tub, over a vanity or in a shower area. Sketch the room and mark the existing light points.

Ask each contractor which location rating the quoted fixtures need, whether the switch and any fan are included, and who repairs the ceiling or wall finish if a new light point is added.

What should the assessment establish?

The contractor confirms that each fixture for a damp room is marked for its exact position and that the switch sits where it suits the room. Existing wiring in a Courtice bathroom may be reused, and the scope says so, or new points may be added and listed; either way, it records who decided. Fixtures, switches and finishing work all appear, with any item you are buying yourself named. Where the room backs onto a shared wall, the repair of any opening in it is described. Set the quotes side by side on those points before looking at totals.

Ontario electrical safety

ESA says almost all electrical work needs a notification of work. If you hire someone for home electrical work, use a Licensed Electrical Contractor. Agree who files the notification and request the Certificate of Acceptance after ESA review.

Read the current ESA notification and inspection guidance, contractor hiring guidance. You can verify an electrical contractor with ESA before agreeing to work.

Questions before you agree to work

Can any ceiling light be used above a bathroom shower?

Not necessarily. Fixtures are marked for the locations they suit, and a damp or wet position needs a matching marking. Ask the contractor to confirm the marking on each chosen product before it is bought.

Who supplies the fixtures when quotes for bathroom lighting differ?

Quotes may assume the contractor supplies them, or that you do. State which in writing, because a quote priced on supplied fixtures and one priced on your own are not like for like.
